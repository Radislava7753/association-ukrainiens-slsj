About us

A living community
at the heart of Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean

Community gathering — Saguenay, 2025

"We are not just an organization — we are a circle of people who support each other, grow together, and create a Ukrainian life in Saguenay and Lac-Saint-Jean."

Founded and registered on January 12, 2026, the Association of Ukrainians of Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean is a non-profit legal entity incorporated under the laws of Québec. We are a member of the Ukrainian Canadian Congress (UCC), which brings together all Ukrainian organizations across Canada.

In a short time, we have become a recognized presence in the region, representing the Ukrainian community with elected officials, institutions and the general public.

Unite
Provide a space for meeting, support and solidarity for all Ukrainian families in the region, whether long-established or newly arrived.
Present
Showcase Ukrainian culture — language, gastronomy, music, traditions — to our Québécois neighbours and in public spaces across the region.
Help
Support Ukrainians in their integration, advocate for their rights with government bodies and contribute to humanitarian efforts in Ukraine.
Our objectives under the Association's bylaws
  • Meet the social, cultural and educational needs of members
  • Support members in adapting to life in the SLSJ region
  • Foster integration into the cultural and social life of the region
  • Present and promote Ukrainian culture and traditions
  • Collaborate with Ukrainian, Québécois and international organizations
  • Represent the Ukrainian community before decision-makers

Our activities in 2025–2026

Concrete actions,
moments that leave a mark

Since our founding, we have organized events that bring people together, educate and move hearts. Here are some highlights from our first year of activity.

Feb. 28, 2026
Merci Borsch — Thank You Saguenay

A gratitude event open to all, where we offered free traditional Ukrainian borsch — recognized by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage. Between 100 and 200 people gathered around a meal and a message: thank you for welcoming us.

Nov. 2025
Holodomor Memorial Ceremony

A commemorative ceremony for victims of the Holodomor, the Ukrainian genocide of 1932–33. Candle lighting, a moment of silence and a historical presentation to raise local community awareness of this little-known chapter of history.

May 21, 2026
Vyshyvanka Day

The vyshyvanka, traditional Ukrainian embroidered garment, is worn worldwide on the third Thursday of May. We organized a gathering in vyshyvanky through the streets of Saguenay — a living symbol of cultural continuity across generations.

Dec. 2025
Founding Great Assembly

The first large gathering of the Ukrainian community of the region, held in December 2025 even before official registration. A founding moment that brought together dozens of families to build the foundations of our Association together.

Our regular activities throughout the year
Ukrainian lessons for children French conversation clubs Board meetings Koliada (Ukrainian Christmas carols) Ukrainian Easter Nature hikes and kayaking Support for newcomers Political representation

Why your contribution matters

Your donation funds
specific, measurable actions

All contributions received are used exclusively for the Association's activities. No profit can be distributed to members. Here is how your support translates into concrete actions for the community.

📚
Education and language programs

Fund Ukrainian lessons for children and French conversation clubs for adults. Preserving the mother tongue while facilitating integration — two essential priorities for our families.

From $25 · Helps 1 child attend a course
🌻
Public cultural events

Organize free events for the entire population of Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean — borsch, vyshyvanka, Koliada, Ukrainian Easter. These moments of encounter build lasting bridges between communities.

From $50 · Covers hall rental for a workshop
🕯️
Direct humanitarian aid in Ukraine

At Merci Borsch, we supported Dereck Blouin-Perry, a Québécois volunteer working on the front lines in Ukraine. His urgent need: $2,000 for video laryngoscopes. Donations like yours save lives.

From $100 · Contributes to emergency medical equipment
🏛️
Rights advocacy and representation

We actively work to obtain pathways to permanent residency for Ukrainians who arrived under the CUAET program. This representation work with elected officials requires travel and resources.

From $200 · Funds an outreach with an elected official
